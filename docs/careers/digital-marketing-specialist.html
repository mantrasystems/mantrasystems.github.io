Digital Marketing Specialist

A unique opportunity to spearhead digital marketing for a leading medical device consultancy.

Job title
Digital Marketing Specialist
Contract
Full-time/ Permanent
Salary
£30-35K based on experience
Location
Sheffield, UK

We are not currently recruiting for this role

  1. Careers

About the position

We’re seeking a Digital Marketing Specialist to join our innovative and dynamic company, operating within an enormous global B2B market with almost unlimited growth potential.

As a highly motivated individual with ideas, creativity and drive, you’ll have the ability to directly influence our growth trajectory, attracting new business and enhancing our market position through data-driven improvements. You’ll work with an exceptional, multidisciplinary team in a modern, collaborative environment where everyone has a voice. Your work will build upon our existing success as a leading regulatory consultancy, serving medical device manufacturers developing pioneering MedTech innovations across 5 continents.

About Mantra Systems

Mantra Systems has established a reputation as one of the leading providers of medical device regulatory consulting services. We serve a global client base from one of the world’s largest industries, with exposure to an enormous range of medical technologies and healthtech innovators.

Operating in a highly regulated industry, medical device manufacturers need expert guidance to successfully navigate regulatory approval requirements and bring their products to market. Our work delivers this expertise through tailored consulting and professional medical writing services. We are a valued partner for over 350 medical device firms around the world.

This position is for:

Applicants who are excited to apply their skills to the marketing of our services to prospects developing pioneering, life-changing technologies. We welcome applicants looking for a vibrant, stimulating and dynamic environment.

The role is suited to those seeking an opportunity to showcase and develop their abilities as a Digital Marketing Specialist within a large, expanding and global B2B market. You’d be well suited if you welcome the chance to innovate and be creative with modern marketing techniques, free from the relative restrictions of working in a large organisation.

We’re looking for a self-starter who would thrive working in an environment that encourages ideas, innovation and fresh approaches. A great work ethic, impeccable integrity and a commitment to the highest quality are essential requirements.

We value personal attributes over direct experience. If you have a deep desire to learn, a positive attitude, ideas, hunger, and a passion to support leading work delivered by an exceptional team, Mantra Systems offers a rare environment in which you will flourish.

This role isn’t a great fit if:

  • You use unguided AI as a substitute for personal endeavor
  • You feel that “good enough” is good enough
  • You need regular and direct oversight, or others to keep you accountable
  • You’re looking for a highly structured, repetitive environment. We move fast, change often, and expect a lot of ownership.

Key tasks

  • Use of social media (including LinkedIn & Sales Navigator) for prospecting
  • Production of graphics & images to support content output
  • Ad campaign management on LinkedIn & other platforms
  • Email marketing
  • Re-marketing and re-engagement
  • Collaboration on the company website and content production
  • Use of success metrics and conversion optimisation
  • Promotional event marketing
  • Continuous brand application & evolution in all company outputs
  • Collaboration on marketing strategy and campaign planning

Essential requirements

  • Proactive & self-starting
  • Proficient in using IT tools and content platforms, including LinkedIn. Experience with Sales Navigator is preferred
  • Ability to produce high quality creative assets using your preferred graphics software
  • Desire to learn and collaborate with our medical & regulatory team
  • Ability to work with tone-of-voice and branding documents
  • Flawless written English with a keen eye for detail
  • A team player with superb verbal communication skills
  • Ability to work efficiently under deadlines

This role will be office-based working from the company HQ in Sheffield. While some home-working will be available, this position is not open to remote candidates.

This role does not require specific experience in medical devices or regulation and is open to candidates who have a range of professional backgrounds.

There will be 3-stages to this selection process. Long-listed applicants will be invited to an informal video call and be given a short-written exercise to submit thereafter. Following completion, successful candidates will then be invited to our office for a final interview.

Benefits

Our benefits are designed to support both your work and your life outside of it. From practical perks to personal growth opportunities, we aim to make your time with us rewarding, enjoyable, and valuable for your future.
