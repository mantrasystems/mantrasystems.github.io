Mantra Systems at MEDICA 2025

  • By Megan Allen
  • Regulatory Medical Writer
  • Share on LinkedIn
  • Share on Facebook
  • Share by email

The Mantra Systems Team are pleased to be sending a delegation MEDICA 2025 in Düsseldorf, Germany, on 18th and 19th November. MEDICA is the world’s largest medical trade fair, which has been running for over 40 years.

MEDICA provides the exciting experience of showcasing medical innovations with several thousand exhibitors from almost 70 nations.

Exhibitors range in a variety of medical fields, such as;

  • Imaging and diagnostics
  • Physiotherapy and rehabilitation equipment
  • Medical devices and in vitro diagnostic devices

From wound care products to artificial intelligence and wearables, MEDICA will host exhibitors with a variety of medical devices across the world. MEDICA also have a Start-Up park where new companies can showcase their developments.

As specialists in regulatory compliance, we’re dedicated to understanding the evolving needs of our customers and the wider medical device industry. Our visit to MEDICA reflects that commitment to listen, learn and engage with innovators shaping the future of medical devices.

Understanding the scope of the medical device field is not just important for medical device regulation and approval, it is also important to understand how devices are developing and how they can improve patient quality of life.

This year, several members of the Mantra Systems team will be attending. Here’s what each of them is most looking forward to:

“I am looking forward to an opportunity to exchange ideas with members of the medical device community from every corner of the world. Also looking forward to catching up with customers and partners past and present, and to building new relationships for the future.”
Paul Hercock, CEO

“Excited for my first visit to MEDICA! I’m looking forward to exploring the latest developments in medical technology, connecting with inspiring professionals, and seeing firsthand how ideas are shaping healthcare 😊”
Kamiya Crabtree, Regulatory Medical Writer

“I'm looking forward to connecting with our existing partners as well as expanding our partner network so that we can help connect our clients with high quality services in the medical device industry. I'm also excited to experience German hospitality with my colleagues!”
Will Bramley, Lead Medical Writer

“Excited to be at MEDICA 2025! Looking forward to meeting amazing innovators from around the world, learning about new technologies, and exploring how we can connect and support China's fast-growing MedTech innovators with global opportunities.”
Ronghe Xu, Regulatory Medical Writer and Strategic BD Lead China

“I’m thrilled to be attending MEDICA for the first time! I’ve heard so much about the sheer size of the event, so many halls filled with innovative devices, and I can’t wait to see it all for myself. I’m especially looking forward to sparking conversations with people from all over the world who are involved in bringing medical devices to market. From design and development to regulatory strategy, this is a unique opportunity to exchange ideas and be inspired by the latest in healthcare innovation.”
Shen May Khoo, Regulatory Project Lead

“I’m excited to connect with professionals from all walks of life within the Medical Device industry! In particular, I look forward to meaningful conversations around the challenges manufacturers and developers are facing when bringing their devices to market, and the opportunities we have to help more devices get to market quicker by overcoming these hurdles.”
Peter Boxall, Lead Medical Writer

Our team members are excited to attend MEDICA and gain insight into the ever-evolving world of medical devices and key challenges facing manufacturers. And of course, we’re looking forward to enjoying some amazing German beer and food! If you see any of our team members around, please don’t hesitate to come and say hello!

We look forward to meaningful conversations and new collaborations that drive safety, compliance, and innovation across the medical device industry.
